A Gate7 target key can occur in an absolute-state, baseline or innovation object; the enclosing field is part of its meaning. The model output is the innovation. The recovered source constructs absolute target and innovation through separate floating-point expressions. Subtracting the serialized absolute and baseline values is **not guaranteed bitwise equal** to the separately serialized innovation; the exact historical serialization must be authenticated for a byte-level claim. Do not use a field rename or naive subtraction as an exact crosswalk. No numeric mapping has been added.

## Source comparison

The recovered mechanics authority defines the force quantity as the time average of gross vertical ground-reaction force over concentric onset through takeoff, divided by the explicit accelerated system mass: `integral(F_z dt)/(m*T_con)`, in N/kg. It defines relative concentric net impulse as `integral(F_z - m*g) dt/m`, in m/s. The original rules use a single explicit system mass, interpolate event boundaries, integrate on the in-contact interval and exclude post-takeoff samples; body mass is not an authorized silent substitute for system mass. The original criterion is the arithmetic mean of exactly three valid trials. The baseline is the arithmetic mean of the two latest valid qualifying pre-index assessments in the inclusive 24–240-hour window.

The run-bound Gate7 split manifest identifies a final public projection. The authenticated source tree also contains a pre-final shadow implementation and a separate earlier public generator. This metadata-only audit did not authenticate the exact invocation or every generator step that materialized the run-bound split; equations in those sources are not proof that every stored row has a fully traceable measurement signal. In particular, a nine-decimal rounding rule in the separate earlier generator is **not** attributed here to the Gate7 historical split.

The public camp task likewise describes additive baseline-relative outputs, N/kg and m/s units, the two-assessment 24–240-hour baseline, H72 at 66–78 hours and D7 at 156–180 hours. Its reconstructed camp target uses a three-trial arithmetic mean. These are agreements in stated structure, not proof that every supplied canonical scalar was measured using the original force signal, phase, weight subtraction and mass definition. The canonical generic outcome definitions alone do not specify those missing mechanics. The different phase-consistent post-exposure construction belongs to another task and cannot supply Gate7 authority by name.

The recovered historical row contract distinguishes participant, origin and query keys; each origin has separate horizon queries with target order and units. The public builder has its own origin/query representation. Matching horizon labels or row position does not authenticate a pairing across datasets. Before scoring, verify the dataset's identity linkage, origin, query, horizon, target window, target order and row pairing against the source authority. Preserve the predeclared target convention and fail closed if any definition is absent or changed.

## Minimum acquisition and authority package

Before outcome access or evaluation, obtain and authenticate: (1) a permission-cleared independent labeled cohort and disclosure terms; (2) a common participant identity linkage proving separation from all Gate7 training and validation groups; (3) query-level origin, horizon, target time, measurement and complete pre-origin observation identities; (4) original-compatible explicit system mass, concentric event and gross/net signal definitions, three valid criterion trials, and the two qualifying baseline assessments; (5) the full 39-feature projection, four history tokens, horizon and independently authenticated five-value basis and row pairing wherever each model needs them; and (6) missingness, exclusions, site/time/population coverage and independently authorized output-summary rules. Test and document the target crosswalk against these source definitions before any independent scoring.

Lock the candidate, baselines, four-cell criteria, uncertainty method, multiplicity and group-aware power plan before unblinding. Establish a data steward, provenance and permission for each dataset and any released summaries. Separate a new synthetic sample from a genuinely independent human cohort and report any coverage or distribution shifts. The existing protocol's no-go conditions apply until these requirements are met.
