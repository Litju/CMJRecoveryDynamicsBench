# Owner submission checklist — later decisions

- Confirm author names, order, affiliations, ORCIDs, corresponding-author contact and CRediT contributions; repository citation metadata does not establish consent.
- Supply funding and competing-interest declarations, including explicit no-funding/no-conflict statements only when true.
- Review and approve the AI-assistance disclosure and take responsibility for scientific content.
- Approve the publication-safe export list; exclude evidence/private and original artifacts.
- Select a venue and make only the verified template adaptation. Recheck current author rules, fees/waivers and data/code availability before submission.
- For SoftwareX, use the verified current template and resolve its literal README.md/Licence.txt expectations separately from the unchanged release.
- Do not submit immediately to JOSS without documented qualifying six-month public development history.
- Confirm availability wording preserves private checkpoint restrictions. Do not promise public replay of private inference.
- No preprint, submission, DOI, release or outreach has occurred. Such actions require separate owner authorization.

Inherited scientific limitations—including historical CUDA failures, partial materialization authority, absent athlete validation and manual NVDA acceptance—remain reportable limitations rather than missing manuscript observations.
