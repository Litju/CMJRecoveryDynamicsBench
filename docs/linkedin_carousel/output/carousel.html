A controlled synthetic study

From Force Plate to Forecast

Modeling CMJ performance changes following training

We define what a countermovement jump (CMJ) measures, model how it changes after a training exposure, describe nine original neural architecture families, and evaluate four reference forecasters on simulated data.

No athlete was measured. Every number traces to the public, version-controlled study results.

2 · Forecast target

From three trials to one target

\Delta F=\bar A^{F}_{\text{criterion}}-\bar A^{F}_{\text{baseline}}\quad[\text{N/kg}] \Delta J=\bar A^{J}_{\text{criterion}}-\bar A^{J}_{\text{baseline}}\quad[\text{m/s}]
  • Each assessment is the mean of three trials; the change is computed from the unrounded means.
  • An additive change, never a percentage and never pooled across units.

3 · Mathematical model

A two-mode response model

A^{\text{true}}_{\text{criterion}}=A^{\text{true}}_{\text{baseline}}\,\bigl(1+r(\tau)+e\bigr) r(\tau)=-a_f\,e^{-\tau/24\,\text{h}}-a_s\,e^{-\tau/84\,\text{h}}
  • 24 h and 84 h are fixed modes of the synthetic generator: modeling assumptions, not measured human recovery constants.
  • Amplitudes are bounded (af 0.01–0.08, as 0–0.08 of baseline); e is a bounded discrepancy.

7 · Uncertainty

How certain is the ordering?

Per-model uncertainty within each evaluation root. These intervals are not a paired ridge-versus-structured comparison: no direct ridge-versus-structured interval or significance result was recorded.

  • Ridge's interval lay below the structured comparator's in every root: a descriptive pattern, not a tested difference.
  • Each model's paired difference from the zero baseline was entirely below 0 in every root.

8 · Diagnostics

What carried the signal

  • In this generator, current exposure and prior history carried the usable signal; participant context added almost nothing.
  • Error barely moved from 24 to 96 training camps, with the penalty held fixed.
  • Every selected penalty sat at the lower edge of the grid (0.01), so weaker regularization is untested.
