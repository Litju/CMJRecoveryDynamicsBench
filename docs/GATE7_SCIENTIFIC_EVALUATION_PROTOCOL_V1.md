# Gate7 scientific evaluation and data readiness protocol, version 1

Status: protocol and synthetic contract checks only. No new historical or independent scores have been computed. This protocol does not authorize evaluation, data access, model publication, clinical use, or retrospective alteration of the historical selection or scoring rule. A study-specific analysis plan must be locked and authorized before outcome access. CPU source parity is not predictive validation; M4–M8 CPU-to-saved-CUDA comparisons failed, and an authentic historical GPU replay is not established.

## Estimand and identity

For each origin, predict the additive innovation from the pre-index baseline in (1) relative mean concentric force, N/kg, and (2) relative concentric net impulse, m/s, in that order. Both outputs are baseline-relative changes, not absolute values or percentage changes. Evaluate H72 (nominal 72 hours, window 66–78) and D7 (nominal 168 hours, window 156–180), yielding four target × horizon cells. Establish the source-defined baseline from the two latest valid qualifying pre-index assessment summaries in the 24–240 hour interval; apply the authenticated assessment-selection, measurement, unit and target-window conventions. Fix missingness, multiple valid target selection, exclusions and query identities in the study plan. Do not substitute one target, horizon or unit for another.

Before any predictions or outcome access, record dataset and source identities, provenance and permission, participant identity linkage, observation and label extraction, query order, target/units, cohort inclusion, site/date coverage, split identities, checkpoint and run-bound preprocessing identities, code/version and environment. Pin these in a restricted study manifest. Observations, dataset keys, fitted values, checkpoints, individual predictions and restricted identities are not public artifacts merely because code is public.

## Candidate comparisons and baselines

All original M0–M8 candidates and final repeats r0/r1 are identified before independent testing. The primary confirmatory candidate must be selected using the historical training/validation evidence only, before access to independent test outcomes; lock both selection and failure handling. Report r0 and r1 separately. The primary family estimate is the arithmetic mean of the two **per-repeat cell metrics**, not a prediction ensemble; never fit, refit, select, or reweight repeats using test outcomes. For uncertainty, resample the same participants and query identities jointly across both repeats and baselines, then recompute the prespecified family metric. Report per-repeat disagreement as a stability diagnostic, not a confidence interval or an estimated predictive distribution.

Prespecify zero innovation (predict the pre-index baseline, hence zero change) as the common primary comparator. A constant innovation baseline may be learned only from the designated training participants with the same target/horizon contract; freeze and identify it before test access. The original Gate5 OLS comparator may be used only when its stored model, rights, input contract and the **same** independent evaluation rows are authorized and authenticated. Its original training data differ from Gate7's, so it is an external comparator, not a matched-training comparison. P8L6 has a different input/training identity and no established validation score: it is not an assumed comparable baseline. All comparisons require identical observations, targets, origins, horizons and exclusions.

Report RMSE, MAE and signed bias (prediction minus observed innovation) in native units for each cell, candidate and repeat, including row and distinct-participant counts. Missing cells, mismatched or duplicate query keys, non-finite values and unit disagreement fail the calculation. Do not silently replace a missing cell with zero, omit it, pool incompatible units, or compute calibrated historical scores with unverified floor/reference parameters. Reproducing the original calibrated score requires the same dataset, query origins, four cells, floor and locked calibration curve; these native-unit research errors are separate.

For each prespecified cell and paired baseline comparison, calculate a 95% participant-cluster bootstrap confidence interval, sampling participants with replacement and retaining all of each sampled participant's origins, horizons, outcomes, repeats and paired model predictions together. Use at least 10,000 resamples with a locked seed and prespecified interval method. Predeclare the analysis unit, minimum group counts and a group-aware sample-size/power plan; a bootstrap cannot compensate for a nonrepresentative or insufficient sample. Small subgroups receive counts and uncertainty, not a claim of absence of harm. Compare distributions by participant, site, time and known load/support features; predefine meaningful subgroups, exclusions, and uncertainty estimates without using held-out labels to redefine them.

For the one locked confirmatory candidate, treat its four cells as a family and control family-wise error at 0.05 using Holm adjustment of prespecified paired tests; report adjusted results and interval estimates. If multiple candidate families are confirmatorily tested, include every candidate × cell claim in the prespecified multiplicity family or use an explicitly locked hierarchical gate. Other post-hoc comparisons are exploratory and labeled as such. Do not choose a winner from the independent test set and present its unadjusted result as confirmatory.

These original models return point innovations, not predictive distributions. Repeat spread is not an individual prediction interval. If a separately justified probabilistic method is authorized and fitted on training data only, predeclare its calibration/validation split, proper scores, coverage, sharpness and subgroup calibration; assess it on untouched test groups. Do not fabricate uncertainty intervals from the two model repeats or tune uncertainty on the test set.

## Split, provenance and leakage gates

Split by linked participant identity so training, validation and independent test groups are pairwise disjoint, including all sessions, origins and horizon queries. Authenticate common identity mapping, resolve aliases before splitting, and audit duplicate records, near-duplicates, shared source episodes, site/time relationships, missingness and out-of-range values. An additional independent cohort or time/site holdout should reflect the intended use population; the same historical split is not independent replication. Inspect distribution shift in covariates and availability, including gaps in history and exposure support, without using test outcomes to change models or preprocessing.

The original checkpoint's fitted preprocessing is used exactly as authenticated and is never recomputed on validation or test data. A new baseline's preprocessing or constant is fit on training groups only. At each prediction origin, only observations available before origin and plans demonstrably known at origin are allowed. Do not substitute later plan revisions, realized future exposure, target labels, future assessments or unavailable history/basis values. Establish M6–M8 basis construction and row pairing against independent authority. The split guard in `metrics.evaluation_readiness` checks supplied identifiers for malformed, missing and overlapping groups; it cannot discover aliases, prove independence, prevent all temporal leakage, or authorize the data. The four-cell error helper verifies numeric/cell contracts, but the caller must independently authenticate query pairing, units and membership.

## Go/no-go rules

**No-go before unblinding** if independent labeled data, authorization, participant linkage and separation, source-defined inputs, fixed preprocessing, locked primary candidate/baseline, exclusion and missingness rules, group-aware power plan, multiplicity family, or all four cells are missing. Prespecify in native units, with domain justification, a practically meaningful improvement margin and an acceptable bias/harm bound for each cell; no number in this protocol is a validated clinical threshold. Lock these quantities before access to test labels. If they are not specified, the evaluation cannot claim success.

**Research go** requires all four primary candidate-versus-zero-baseline paired RMSE differences to meet their locked improvement margins with the prespecified group-aware uncertainty and Holm-adjusted criterion; MAE, signed bias and their uncertainty must satisfy the locked harm/bias bounds in every cell. Require sufficient prespecified group counts and no unresolved leakage, missing-cell, provenance or distribution-support failures. Report failures and uncertainty for every cell and repeat. A statistically significant but practically negligible gain, an underpowered or shifted test, an exploratory result, or a successful synthetic fixture is not a go. This is a research-evaluation decision only: clinical deployment, prescribed interventions, robustness outside the evaluated populations and production readiness require additional independent evidence and approvals.
